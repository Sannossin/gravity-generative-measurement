**Specific or shared?** Two predictions are specific to the framework: the full dark-energy curve with no free parameter (bet μ0 = 4/3), and r ≈ 0 (no graviton) together with the Starobinsky n_s (other low-energy inflations also give r ≈ 0, but with a different n_s). The value of n_s is shared by any Starobinsky model with the same reheating; the absence of entanglement is shared by classical-gravity models; the (w0, wa) position lies close to the "mirage" line common to many models.
